🌿 EcoPulse — Smart Energy Dashboard

A modern responsive UI template for smart energy monitoring, renewable energy analytics, battery management, and AI-powered energy insights.

📌 About EcoPulse

EcoPulse is a modern Smart Energy Dashboard UI designed to help users monitor and understand their energy consumption and renewable energy production.

The interface combines:

☀️ Solar energy monitoring
⚡ Energy consumption tracking
🔋 Battery status
📊 Energy analytics
🌱 Carbon emission tracking
🤖 AI-powered recommendations
🔔 Energy alerts
📈 Energy forecasting

The design focuses on creating a clean, modern, and easy-to-understand energy management experience.

🎯 UI Pattern

Selected UI Topic: Energy & Smart Sustainability Dashboard

EcoPulse follows the dashboard UI pattern, where important information is presented through cards, charts, metrics, status indicators, and interactive controls.

The dashboard provides users with an overview of their energy system without requiring them to navigate through multiple pages.

🔎 Topic Research

Modern energy-management interfaces commonly use dashboard-based layouts to present large amounts of information in a simple visual format.

Some common interface patterns include:

KPI cards for important measurements
Real-time status indicators
Interactive charts
Energy production and consumption visualizations
Battery monitoring
Carbon footprint indicators
Alerts and notifications
Forecasting interfaces
AI-generated recommendations
Responsive layouts for different screen sizes
Why this UI pattern is relevant

Renewable energy systems generate a large amount of information, including production, consumption, storage, and environmental data.

A dashboard allows users to quickly understand this information through visual indicators instead of reading large amounts of raw data.

EcoPulse applies these patterns to create a more engaging and user-friendly smart-energy experience.

✨ What Makes EcoPulse Different?

Instead of creating a basic analytics dashboard, EcoPulse combines energy monitoring with AI-assisted insights.

🚀 How to Run
Option 1 — VS Code Live Server
Open the EcoPulse folder in VS Code.
Open index.html.
Right-click the file.
Select Open with Live Server.
The EcoPulse dashboard will open in your browser.
Option 2 — Browser

You can also open index.html directly in a modern web browser.
